# /brag-slim plan — CINÉ

## Inspect

- **What is it?** CINÉ is a cinema seat-booking app that drops you into a real-time 3D model of the hall, so you see the exact view from a seat before you pay for it.
- **Who is it for?** Anyone booking a film who has ever ended up craning their neck in row A or squinting from the back corner.
- **What sets it apart?** Every seat has its own camera: the view is solved from that chair's real position, eye height and angle, so no two seats look the same. The seat map literally opens through your seat into the hall.
- **Most impressive / funniest claim:** the app's own copy for the front row: *"The screen fills your vision. Expect to look up."* Plus the hard numbers per seat: distance, how much of your vision the screen fills, sightline score.
- **Visual hook:** a giant, steeply tilted screen seen from row A, then the map tearing open into 3D and the camera diving into the chair.
- **Real UI / flow to show:** seat map → hover a taken seat → pick a pair → "See your view" → circle reveal + camera flight into the seat → jump to other seats from the mini map → Confirm → ticket.
- **Tone:** `chaotic` (FAST, LOUD, ALL CAPS), played as a trailer.
- **Share caption:** "I built a cinema booking app that puts you IN the seat before you buy it."

No personal data is shown: the film, cinema, prices and booking reference are all fictional, generated by the app itself.

## Angle

Booking a cinema seat is a gamble. CINÉ removes it by letting you sit in the seat first — live, in 3D. The video is the product doing exactly that, cut like a trailer.

- **Hook:** row A's monstrous tilted screen. "BOOKED ROW A?" → "ENJOY THE NECK PAIN."
- **Highlights:** (1) the map opens into the hall and the camera dives into seat F8 — "THAT'S YOUR ACTUAL VIEW." (2) "EVERY SEAT GETS ITS OWN CAMERA." while flying to the far front corner. (3) "BACK ROW: FULL PICTURE." from K13.
- **Punchline:** booked — the camera pulls back over the house, your seats glow, the ticket rises: "See you there."
- **Outro:** CINÉ, "SEE THE VIEW FROM YOUR SEAT BEFORE YOU BOOK." and the live URL.

## Sound

150 BPM (one beat = 0.4 s = exactly 12 frames at 30 fps), D minor (Dm – B♭ – F – C), resolving to D major for the outro. Bars start on beat 2 (beats 0–1 are a pickup) so the drop, both seat jumps, the booking and the outro all land on a downbeat. Built in numpy as one piece: kick, clap, hats, saw bass with sidechain, supersaw stabs, plucked arp, risers and tuned impacts on the cuts, all on the same beat grid from frame 0, sharing one reverb.
